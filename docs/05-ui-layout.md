# 05 · UI layout and screens

Behaviour reference: open `reference/compliance-os-sun-v2.html` in a browser (it is a self-contained mockup; click around, resize to 390 px) for screens and flows. The look is Vercel's Geist design system as set out in §6; where the mockup's styling differs, §6 wins. Where the mockup shows things outside the MVP scope (dashboards with readiness, Speak-up, partner console, app builder), keep the slot in the menu out of the MVP unless docs/01 lists it.

## 1. Routes (Next.js App Router)

| `/[org]/dashboards/[dashboardId]` | One dashboard: tabs for every dashboard, widget cards |

Every state that matters is in the URL: open item, view, search. Back/forward work. "Copy link" copies the absolute URL.

### 1.1 Your account and two-step sign-in

The words are always "two-step sign-in" and "authenticator app", never "MFA" or "TOTP" (docs/09 §8.2).

- **Your account** (`/account`, from the account menu › Your account; outside any workspace, on the soft background
  like onboarding, one 576 px column, full width with 16 px gutters at 390 px): a back link ("Back to <workspace>"),
  the `h1` "Your account" with the name and email, then the cards **Your name**, **Email address**, **Password**,
  **Two-step sign-in**, **This session** and **Delete my account** (sent by a workspace that requires two-step
  sign-in, Two-step sign-in comes first).
  - **Your name** (owner decision 16 Nov 2026): the name, "People in your workspaces see this name. Your initials
    follow it." and **Change name** (outline), which opens in place **Your name** (focused, the current name) with
    **Save name** and **Cancel** (focus returns to the button). Onboarding's rule: required, trimmed, at most 80
    characters ("Name is required."); the initials are derived from it as onboarding derives them. Saved: the toast
    "Your name is saved." (`changeDisplayName`, the person's own `profiles` row under `profiles_self`; a deleted
    profile is not renamed).
  - **Email address** (docs/09 §12): the address and what it is for, and **Change email** (outline), which opens in
    place "New email address" (focused), **Send confirmation links** and **Cancel** (focus returns to the button).
    "That is already your email address." under the field. Sent: the status line "Waiting for confirmation: <new>.
    We sent a link to <old> and one to <new>. Your email changes once both links are opened. They work for 1 hour."
    and the button reads **Use another address**. Back from the first of the two links (`?email=confirm-other`): "One
    link is confirmed. Open the link we sent to the other address to finish." A link that failed
    (`?email=link-failed`): "That link did not open here: … If the address above has not changed, change it again".
  - **Password**: one line (signed out on other devices afterwards) and **Change password**, which opens in place
    **Current password** (focused), **New password** ("At least 12 characters.") and **Repeat the new password**,
    then **Change password** and **Cancel**. Errors under the fields: "That is not your current password.", "Use at
    least 12 characters.", "The two passwords are not the same." Done: the status line "Your password has been
    changed. You are signed out on your other devices." and focus back on the button. Under it: "Forgot it, or never
    set one? Get a reset link by email" (Forgot password).
- **Email link** (`/auth/confirm`, the sign-in card; docs/09 §12): every auth email (sign-in, confirm sign-up, reset
  password, change of email) links here, so a link scanner that opens the link first does not use it up. "Compliance
  OS", then one heading and one primary button by the link's kind: reset "Set a new password" / **Continue to set a
  new password**; sign-in or sign-up "Sign in to Compliance OS" / **Continue**; email change "Confirm your new email
  address" / **Confirm**. Under the button: "This link works once, for 1 hour." Focus starts on the button (full width
  under 640 px); a second click while the first is on its way does nothing. The button opens the link and goes where
  it leads (Set a new password, the page asked for, Your account); a used or expired link goes where it did before
  (Forgot password `?error=expired`, Your account `?email=link-failed`, `/login?error=link`). A link missing its token
  or with an unknown kind: "This link is not complete" · "Copy the whole link from the email, or ask for a new link."
  and **Back to sign in** (focused). Never indexed, no referrer.
- **Forgot password** (`/login/forgot-password`, the sign-in card; linked as "Forgot password" under the sign-in form):
  "Reset your password", the **Email** field (focused) and **Send reset link**. Whatever the address, the answer is
  "Check your email · If an account uses that address, we have sent a link to reset your password. The link works
  once, for 1 hour." **Back to sign in**. A reset link that failed comes back here, in place of the form, with the
  reason and **Ask for a new link** (focused; back to this form): `?error=link`, a link from Supabase's default email
  opened in another browser than the one that asked (docs/09 §12), "This link has to be opened in the same browser you
  asked from, or it has expired. Ask for a new link."; `?error=expired`, "This link has expired or was already used.
  Ask for a new link."
- **Set a new password** (`/login/new-password`, the sign-in card): "For <email>.", **New password** (focused, "At
  least 12 characters."), **Repeat the new password** and **Set new password**. A person with an authenticator app
  enters its code on the two-step page first, which brings them back here. Done: "Your password has been changed.",
  "You are signed out on your other devices. Use the new password there." and **Continue** (to `next`, else Home).
  - **Two-step sign-in** with a status chip, "On" (a tick, green) or "Off", and one line on what it does. When on, the
    apps as rows ("Authenticator app, Added 15 Nov 2026"; numbered when there are several) with **Remove**, which opens
    an inline confirm ("Remove this app? Two-step sign-in turns off …" for the last one, naming the workspaces that
    require it; **Remove app** in red and **Keep it**, which has focus). Then **Set up two-step sign-in** (black) or
    **Add another authenticator app**. Setting up shows three numbered steps in place: 1. the QR code (180 px, alt
    text "QR code to scan with your authenticator app") with app examples and the name it adds ("Compliance OS
    (<email>)"); 2. the key in groups of four (`text-label-13-mono`) with **Copy key**; 3. "Enter the 6-digit code
    the app shows" (focused, numeric, one-time-code autocomplete), then **Turn on two-step sign-in** and **Cancel**.
    A wrong code says "That code is not right. Enter the code the app shows now." under the field and focuses it
    again. Done: the toast "Two-step sign-in is on.", the chip turns On, and **If you lose your phone**: add a spare
    app on another device, or keep the key in a password manager; without either, the workspace owner contacts
    support to reset it. There are no recovery codes.
  - **This session**: "Signed in since 15 Nov 2026, 14:31, with two-step sign-in." (Supabase does not say when a
    session ends), a line that sessions end some hours after sign-in and sooner when away, and **Sign out**.
  - Sent by a workspace that requires it (`?required=<slug>`): a warning line above the cards, "<Workspace> requires
    two-step sign-in. Set it up below to open the workspace.", and **Continue to the workspace** once it is set up.
  - **Delete my account** (last; docs/09 §12): what happens ("You lose access to every workspace and can no longer
    sign in. Your name is removed. The records you made stay in each workspace and are shown as made by “Former
    member”. This cannot be undone.") and **Delete my account** (outline, red text), which opens the Dialog "Delete
    your account?": the same in short, then "Type <email> to confirm" (focused; the account's address, any case),
    **Cancel** and **Delete my account** (red). A different address: "Type your email address exactly as it is
    shown." under the field. The only owner of a workspace is refused in the dialog: "You are the only owner of
    <Workspace>. Make someone else owner of <Workspace> or delete it first." (several: "… of A and B. Make someone
    else owner of each of them, or delete them, first."). A person who still owes their authenticator app's code
    goes to the two-step page and back to the open dialog (`?delete=open`). Done: signed out, on `/account-deleted`
    (the sign-in card): "Your account has been deleted.", that the records stay without the name, and **Go to sign
    in**.
- **Two-step sign-in at sign-in** (`/login/two-step`, the sign-in card): "Two-step sign-in", "Enter the 6-digit code
  from your authenticator app for <email>.", a "Which app?" radio list when there are several, the **6-digit code**
  field (focused; spaces allowed) and **Continue**. Under a rule: "Lost your phone? …" and **Sign in as someone else**
  (signs out). A person with an app who signed in with a password or email link reaches nothing else until the code is
  entered: every page sends them here with `next`, every command answers "Enter the code from your authenticator app
  to finish signing in."
- **Workspace admin › Security** (owners and admins): the card **Two-step sign-in** with the switch "Require two-step
  sign-in for this workspace · On/Off" and what it does. Owners change it; an owner who does not use two-step sign-in
  yet sees the switch off and disabled with "Set up two-step sign-in for yourself first. Open Your account"; admins
  see it disabled with "Only owners can change this." Toasts: "Two-step sign-in is now required." / "… now
  optional." While it is on, a member whose session did not use two-step sign-in is sent to Your account with
  `?required=` from every page of the workspace, and its commands are refused with "This workspace requires two-step
  sign-in. Set it up in Your account, then try again."

## 2. App shell

The shell and pages use the Geist design system (§6): a Vercel-style side menu, Geist grays, flat bordered cards.

```
┌──────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ side menu    │ top bar: [search……………… /]                   [Inbox 3] [MP] │
│ 256px        ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ bg-200       │ page: crumb · h1 · description · actions, underline tabs  │
└──────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 Side menu
- 256 px, background `--subtle` (Geist background-200), right border `--line`, full height, sticky. A `nav` landmark "Side menu".
- **Top:** workspace switcher (initials on a black circle, name, ⇅: the user's workspaces, New workspace), then **Create** for editors, admins and owners (the same role check as Build; viewers and auditors do not see it, on desktop or phone: owner decision 15 Nov 2026). There is no menu filter: the one search is the top bar's (§2.3, `/`), which finds sheets and items (owner decision 15 Nov 2026, board review UX-5).
- **Four groups** (owner decision 15 Nov 2026), each with a 12 px heading:
  - **Work:** Home, My work (count badge = overdue + requests to me + approvals waiting for me, worded "n waiting" for screen readers), then the installed packs' apps the person's role may see (docs/13; a list named "Apps").
  - **Registers:** one heading for every sheet. First the domain folders of the shared registers (docs/11 Core registers; `src/components/shell/menu-registers.tsx`) in their order (Management system, Risk and controls, Operations, Privacy and security, Suppliers), each collapsible (library icon, name, chevron; closed ones remembered like folders) with its sheets: the shared registers' and the sheets standards put there. A domain without a live sheet is not shown (Privacy and security and Suppliers stay hidden until a standard fills them). Domains are not renamed, moved or archived from the menu and sheets are not dragged into them. Then the workspace's own folders (`src/components/shell/menu-sheets.tsx`): one collapsible folder per folder (folder icon, name, chevron; closed folders remembered per browser; the folder of the open sheet always opens), its sheets indented with their two-letter code in a small mono box, then "Other sheets" (sheets without a folder), then "+ New sheet". The "+" on the Registers heading is below. Empty state (no sheets at all): "No sheets yet." and "Create your first sheet".
  - **Reports:** Dashboards, Evidence library, Audit outputs. Every role, auditors included.
  - **Build:** Workflow, Automations, Design (§4), Workspace admin. Editors, admins and owners see Build; Workspace admin only admins and owners. Viewers and auditors never see Build (the pages keep their own role checks).
- **Organising folders and sheets** (docs/02 §13; `src/components/shell/menu-sheets.tsx`). Viewers see none of these controls.
  - **"+" on the Registers heading** (editors; 24 px, shown on hover or focus with a mouse, always on touch screens and under 760 px): New sheet, New folder (owners and admins: a folder row appears at the top with an inline name field, placeholder "Folder name", and the note "Folders organise sheets. To choose who sees a folder's sheets, use Access in its menu."), Import CSV.
  - **"…" on a folder:** New sheet here (opens the wizard with that folder chosen); for owners and admins also Rename, Move up, Move down, Sort A–Z (once), Access… and Archive (a dialog lists the folder's sheets: "Archive folder and sheets").
  - **Access** (P2, docs/02 §16; `src/components/access/access-panel.tsx`): a dialog from the folder's and the sheet's "…" menu (owners and admins), the sheet header's Access button (everyone who can read it) and Sheet settings › Access. It shows a status line ("Restricted. Only the 3 people listed, and the workspace owners. You: can edit."), the choice "Same as <folder> / Everyone in the workspace / Only people I choose" (folders: the last two), the people with their workspace role and a level select (No access, Can edit, Can comment, Can view; viewers and auditors only Can view; owners "Always has access"), and the latest 20 access changes. People who cannot manage it see the same, read-only, with "Only owners, and admins who have access, can change who has access."
  - **Restricted in the menu:** a lock after the name of a restricted folder or sheet ("Restricted" for screen readers). Owners and admins who are not listed see a restricted sheet's name muted with "Restricted", not a link; its address shows "This sheet is restricted" with who to ask. Everyone else does not see it at all.
  - **"…" on a sheet** (editors): Rename, Move to… (a dialog with a search field over the folders and "No folder"), Move up, Move down, Duplicate (Copy a sheet with this sheet chosen), Sheet settings; owners and admins also Archive.
  - **Inline rename:** double-click, F2 or the menu; Enter saves, Escape cancels, leaving the field saves.
  - **Drag and drop** (dnd-kit): drag a sheet row (or its grip, shown on hover or focus) between rows, onto a folder header (it goes to the end of that folder) or onto "Other sheets", which shows while a sheet is dragged; owners and admins drag folders. A 2 px `--focus` line shows where it lands; a folder header it would go into gets a ring; a closed folder opens after 600 ms. Keyboard: the grip takes the dnd-kit keyboard sensor (Space or Enter, arrows, Space or Enter, Escape); on a focused row Alt+↑/↓ moves it one place in its level, Alt+→ moves a sheet from "Other sheets" into the folder above it (the last folder), Alt+← moves it out of its folder. A polite live region announces "Risk register moved to Risk and controls, position 2 of 5". Dragging is off when the menu is collapsed (Ctrl+B) and under 760 px (use the menus there).
  - Moves are optimistic: the menu changes at once, the server places the row between the same neighbours; a failure rolls back with a toast "Could not move … Retry".
  - **Empty states:** no folders and no sheets: "No sheets yet." and "Create your first sheet" (editors); an empty folder: muted "No sheets in this folder" and "New sheet here".
  - **Workspace admin, Packs** (owners and admins; docs/11): available packs from the catalogue (title, version, description, "Inside: …" counts and sheet names, an "Installed" chip with a check), each with Preview: a dialog with the planner's words (what is created, folders used or made, sheets with prefix and field counts, links between sheets, renamed ID prefixes, automations as sentences, forms, dashboards, "Good to know" warnings, conflicts that block), Cancel and Install; installing shows a status line and a bar, then "Installed …" with Open (the first sheet) and Done; a failure says nothing changed and offers Try again. Installed packs: version, who installed it and when, Open, Archive with an inline confirm that says what it archives (a standard: "Archives its own sheets and dashboards, turns its automations off and takes its columns, views and empty groups and the ISO 14001 tag off the shared registers of Shared registers. No shared rows are deleted: items it added there, and items tagged with it, stay with their history."; the shared registers cannot be archived while a standard builds on them: "Archive … first: it builds on this pack."). A standard's preview says "Installs Shared registers 1.1.0 (Recommended) first." (or "Adds Everything to Shared registers first.") and lists "Adds to the shared registers": per sheet the columns, groups, example items, automations, views and options it adds; after install "Installed Shared registers 1.1.0 (Recommended) first." Pack activity: the latest 20 `pack.installed` / `pack.archived` entries as sentences.
  - **Workspace admin, Frameworks** (owners and admins; docs/12): adopted frameworks (title, library sheet name, a coverage bar with its words "6% covered (8 of 125) · 16 applicable · 2 not applicable · 109 to decide", Open) and the framework catalogue (title, kind chip, requirement count and publisher, the licence note, Source link, "Adopted" chip or Adopt).   - **Workspace admin, Archived** (owners and admins): archived folders (restored with the sheets archived with them; an empty one can be deleted), archived sheets, and the latest 20 folder and sheet changes from the activity log.

### 3.1 Table view (default)
One view for working through items as rows; it replaced the former List and Grid views. `?view=list` and `?view=grid` (old links, saved defaults) open the Table, and saved views stored with type `list` or `grid` load as Table views (mapped when read; `views.type` stores a Table view as `list` until a migration adds `table`).
- Hint line (dismissible, remembered): "**Click a cell, then click again or press Enter to edit.** Click a name to open the item. **+** adds a column. 🔒 kept by the app."
- **One sticky column header** per group card, muted `text-label-12` names. Sticky name column (checkbox · drag handle · name); the header's checkbox selects every shown item; last header cell "+" adds a field. Locked fields show a lock after the name with the tooltip "Kept by the app. You can hide it, not delete it." **System columns** (docs/11 Core registers: the Core registers' columns and the columns add-ons add to them) show a lock icon after the name whose tooltip and accessible name say "System column. Used by ISO 14001. You can hide or rename it, not delete it or change its type."; their column menu has no Delete, their settings show that sentence in place of the type change and keep Delete field disabled ("A system column. You can hide it in a view, not delete it."). The database refuses the rest (docs/02 Core registers and extends).
- Groups are collapsible sections: a title row tinted with the group colour and a 4 px bar, chevron button (`aria-expanded`), name in the group colour, "n items", a checkbox selecting the group. Collapsed state is UI state.
- Group cards: 1 px `--line` border, 10 px radius, 24 px apart. Title row 48 px (16 px side padding): a 24 px chevron button, the dot and name (14 px semibold), "n items" (mono 12 px semibold, muted on `--soft`), "% done". ## 6. Design system: Geist

The whole app follows Vercel's Geist design system with the stack we already have (Geist Sans and Mono self-hosted, Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui on Radix, lucide-react). No other UI library. Everything below lives in `src/app/globals.css`; components use the tokens and type styles, never hex colours or ad-hoc px font sizes.

**Light only for now.** Every colour is a step on a Geist scale defined on `:root`; the semantic tokens point at those steps. A dark theme later redefines the scales (for example under `[data-theme='dark']`) and nothing else.

### 6.1 Colour

- Scales (light values): `--background-100` (white), `--background-200`; `--gray-100…1000` and `--gray-alpha-100…1000`; `--blue`, `--red`, `--amber`, `--green`, `--teal`, `--purple`, `--pink` each `100…1000`. Tailwind classes exist for every step (`bg-gray-100`, `text-blue-900`, `border-gray-alpha-400` …).
- Semantic tokens (what components use): `--ink` gray-1000 (primary text), `--muted` gray-900 (secondary text: 5.7:1 on white, 5.1:1 on gray-100), `--faint` gray-700 (icons and marks only, never text), `--line` gray-alpha-400 (1 px borders), `--line-strong` gray-alpha-500 (hover borders), `--soft` gray-100 (fills, skeletons, a control's on state), `--subtle` background-200 (quiet fills inside white surfaces: collapsed column stubs, hints), `--sel` blue-300 (selected rows, 1.17:1, always with the ticked checkbox), `--brand` gray-1000 (primary buttons), `--focus` blue-700, `--nav-hover` / `--nav-active` gray-alpha-100 / 200.
- Surfaces (owner decision 17 Nov 2026, Vercel's calm base): `--page` background-200 (the main column and top bar behind every screen), `--sidebar` gray-100 (side menu, phone bottom bar and menu sheet; icons there stay `--muted`, 5.1:1, and `--faint` is not used in it, 2.9:1), `--surface` background-100 (sheet and group cards, lists, panels), `--surface-line` gray-alpha-500 (a sheet card's outline and the header row's bottom line, 1.65:1), `--row-line` gray-alpha-400 (lines between rows, 1.20:1), `--col-line` gray-alpha-100 (lines between columns, 1.12:1), `--head` gray-100 (header row fill; header words are `--muted` in medium weight, 5.1:1), `--row-hover` gray-100 (row hover, 1.12:1; gray-alpha-100 made opaque so sticky cells still cover what scrolls under them). No zebra stripes. Tailwind classes: `bg-page`, `bg-sidebar`, `bg-surface`, `border-surface-line`, `border-b-row-line`, `border-r-col-line`, `bg-head`, `bg-row-hover`.
- States: `--good` green-900, `--warn` amber-900, `--crit` red-900, `--info` blue-900 (links), each with a `-soft` fill (100/200 step); text is ≥ 4.5:1 on white and on its soft fill. `--bar-good` / `--bar-crit` are the bright 700 steps for thin bars beside words.
- Option colours (`--label-<colour>`, stored by name on each option) stay for dots, group bars, Kanban and Calendar; `--solid-<colour>` where white text sits on them. In the Table, options use the tint triplets `--tint-<colour>-bg / -line / -ink`: the label colour mixed with white in oklch at 20 % for the fill, 45 % for the 1 px ring and 27 % for `-hover`, and the colour at 72 % with black for the words (yellow `#854d0e` and grey `#3f3f46` fixed for 4.5:1). Grey, the weakest hue, sits on `--gray-200` with a `--gray-alpha-500` ring. Against white the fills are 1.13 to 1.40:1, the rings 1.32 to 2.25:1 and the words 6.1:1 and up (owner decision 17 Nov 2026, up from 13 / 22 %).
- Meters: `--meter-1…4` (green-700, amber-700, amber-800, red-700) and `--meter-off` (gray-100). Date tracks: `--bar-good` / `--bar-crit`, `--bar-warn` (amber-700, due within 14 days) and `--bar-far` (gray-600). Heat scale `--h1…h4` (fill) / `--h1i…h4i` (text) for the risk heatmap.
- **When colour is allowed:** option and status values, focus, links, selected rows and the semantic states (good, warn, crit, info), always next to text or a symbol that says the same. Everything else is monochrome.
- **Empty values:** an empty cell shows nothing (an outlined box read as an input you must fill). While the row is hovered, an empty option cell shows a dashed `--line-strong` box where a click sets the value; screen readers hear "Empty".
- **Toolbar on state:** a filter, sort, one-group or missing-required control that is on turns `--soft` with a `--faint` border, `--ink` words in medium weight and its count or words ("Sorted by Due date", "Filter 2"). Neutral, never a hue by kind (`toolbarOn` in `toolbar-styles.ts`). Counts on controls sit on gray-alpha-200 so they show on white and on the on state.

### 6.2 Type

Tailwind utilities with Geist's sizes, line heights and tracking (px, independent of the 14 px root). Spacing utilities follow Geist's 4 px grid whatever the root size (`--spacing: 4px`: `p-2.5` is 10 px, `size-4` 16 px). The fonts are the Geist and Geist Mono versions Google Fonts serves (`@fontsource/geist`, `@fontsource/geist-mono`), self-hosted, so text measures the same as in the design.

| Style | Classes | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Heading (600, negative tracking) | `text-heading-72/64/56/48/40/32/26/24/20/16/14` | page `h1` = heading-26 (26 px on 31.2 px, −0.78 px, the board design's page title), card and dialog titles heading-16, KPI numbers heading-32 |
| Label (single line) | `text-label-20/18/16/14/13/12`, `text-label-14-mono/13-mono/12-mono` | controls, cells, menus, meta; ids, codes and counts in mono |
| Label half steps (the board design) | `text-label-12.5/11`, `text-label-12.5-mono/11.5-mono/11-mono/10.5-mono` | 12.5: Table dates and scores; 11: initials in 22–24 px avatars; 11 / 11.5 mono: counts on controls, update pills, key hints; 10.5 mono: "in 65d". The 13.5 and 9.5 steps were rounded to 13 and 11 (board review UX-14): buttons, sheets in the side menu and option boxes use `text-label-13` |
| Copy (running text) | `text-copy-24/20/18/16/14/13`, `text-copy-14-mono/13-mono` | descriptions, help, empty states |

Label and copy set no weight: add `font-medium` (500) or `font-semibold` (600, the heaviest Geist weight we use). `cn()` knows these are font sizes (`src/lib/utils.ts`).

### 6.3 Materials and radius

- The page is `--page` (background-200, #fafafa) and the side menu `--sidebar` (gray-100); everything people read sits on white `--surface` cards on it.
- Cards and sections: white, a 1 px `--line` border, **no shadow**. Sheet cards (the Table's group cards, phone cards, the bordered lists) use the stronger `--surface-line` outline and a `--head` header row with a `--surface-line` bottom line; rows are split by `--row-line` and columns by `--col-line`. Shadows only on floating surfaces: `shadow-tooltip`, `shadow-menu` (popovers, menus, cell editors, search results), `shadow-modal` (dialogs), `shadow-fullscreen` (side sheets, the item panel).
- Radius: `rounded-md` 6 px controls and option boxes, `rounded-lg` 8 px cards, `rounded-xl` 12 px large surfaces (dialogs, the public form), `rounded-full` pills and avatars.

### 6.4 Controls

- Buttons (`src/components/ui/button.tsx`): `default` primary (black, near-white text), `outline` / `secondary` (white, 1 px border), `ghost` / `tertiary` (no border, gray-alpha hover), `destructive` (red), `link`. Sizes: `sm` 32 px, default 40 px, `lg` 48 px; icon buttons the same squares. 150 ms transitions on colour, background and border; `active:scale-[0.98]`. The sheet page follows the board design: header actions 36 px, toolbar controls 38 px (`toolbar-styles.ts`).
- Focus: a 2 px `--focus` (blue) outline 2 px clear of the control, everywhere (`globals.css`); text inputs show a blue border and a 2 px blue ring (`ring-2 ring-focus`) instead, wrapped inputs on their wrapper (`focus-within`); never a border change alone (UX-12). With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` nothing animates: dialogs, side panels, skeletons, progress bars and chevrons show their end state at once (`globals.css`, UX-11).
- Inputs and selects: 40 px (32 px inside editors and dense rows), white, 1 px `--line` border, `--line-strong` on hover, 6 px radius. Menus and popovers: 8 px radius, border, `shadow-menu`, 14 px items with a gray-alpha highlight. Switch: black when on, gray-700 when off, always with its state word. Toasts: dark, bottom centre.
- Option values outside the Table show a colour dot with the label (plus "!" for action needed and a check for done).

### 6.5 Words

Owner decision 15 Nov 2026 (board review UX-7, A6): one noun per thing, in every screen, message, toast and doc. Code identifiers, field keys, columns, manifest ids and activity log action ids do not change: only words people read.

| Word | Means | Do not say |
|---|---|---|
| sheet | a table of items with its fields, views and settings, the workspace's own or from a standard | board, register (except below), list, table (the Table is a view) |
| item | one row of a sheet: what "+ New" adds and the item panel opens | record, entry, row (row only for a line of the Table or a CSV file) |
| sub-item | an item of a sheet's hidden child sheet (docs/02 §12) | child row, task |
| Registers | the side menu's heading over every sheet; "register" also inside a proper name from a standard ("Risk register", "Records of processing") | register for any other sheet |
| standard | a pack that builds on the shared registers (ISO 27001, GDPR …), shown by its plain title | add-on |
| Shared registers | the Core registers pack: the registers every standard builds on | Core, Core registers |
| Basic / Recommended / Everything | how much of a standard to set up: its levels, lowest first (owner decision 15 Nov 2026: Starter reads Recommended, not Standard, because "standard" means a standard such as ISO 27001) | Foundations, Starter, Full; Standard for a level |
| field | a column of a sheet with a type | column (fine for the Table's layout) |
| entry | only a line of the activity log ("14 entries recomputed") | an item |
| Board | only the Kanban view's name | a sheet |

Dates `11 Nov 2026`, times 24 h, sentence case, no exclamation marks (CLAUDE.md). The pack words are mapped at display time (`src/engine/packs/words.ts`: titles drop "(add-on)", "Core registers" reads "Shared registers", the levels read Basic, Recommended and Everything), so the manifests and their versions stay as written.

## 7. States

Every list, table and panel needs: skeleton loading (no layout shift), empty state with one action ("No items yet. Add the first one." + button), error state with Retry, and offline banner ("You are offline. Changes will fail until you reconnect.").
