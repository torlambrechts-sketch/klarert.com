Mockup guide

Menu structure

One workspace per organisation, and the spreadsheet is the foundation. Every register in the sidebar is a board on the same engine. The ISO apps install system boards; customers build their own boards next to them under "Your sheets". Apps, automations, workflows and dashboards all sit on top of the boards.

Everything is a board

On top: apps, workflows, dashboards, portalsISO 9001, 27001, 45001, 14001, NIS2 and Speak-up apps · customer-built apps · CAPA and approval workflows · dashboards · QR forms, auditor room, consultant console
Boards20 system boards installed by the apps, with locked columns, status rules and relations that cannot break, next to the customer's own boards
Board engine: the spreadsheetItems and groups · 14 column types · table, kanban, timeline, calendar and chart views · inline editing · when-if-then automations · forms · cell-level history in a hash-chained audit log

Try it: open Risk register (system board) or Vehicle checks (customer board). Click a status cell, add a column with “+”, add an item, drag a card in Kanban. Set a control to Failing on Controls and the linked risks change on the Risk register. Open “Automate” on any board to build when-if-then rules; workflow steps sit in each item’s Workflow tab. Press “Send report” on the incident form to fire a form trigger.
